06

The tiles at a glance

Battery demandThe starting point: GWh of cells, actual to 2025, forecast to 2030. A licensed, restricted-use source.
Material intensitykg per kWh for each chemistry. The same factors whatever the scenario.
Raw materials requiredDemand × intensity. Change either one and this tile moves.
Supply capacityFive stages from mine to cell, operating today plus announced. Read lesson 3 again before adding the two.
Recycling contributionRecycled supply against the battery rules' minimums. Similar, not identical, measures.
